# Worked examples

These show the decision, the length and the spirit. They are not text to copy. A reply that
lands on the same sentence as an example here is wrong, even when the comment is nearly the
same as the example. If you find yourself writing one of these sentences, write a different
one.

Comment: "Does it cover mushrooms at all?"
{"decision":"reply","reason":"content question about a subject the book deliberately excludes","reply_text":"It leaves fungi out completely and says why, it stays on leaves, fruit and seeds. {{cta_phrase_2}}"}

Comment: "How much is it and where do I get it?"
{"decision":"reply","reason":"clear purchase intent, price and place asked together","reply_text":"{{price_text}}, {{cta_phrase_1}}"}

{"decision":"reply","reason":"warm praise with no question, thank and let them know the book exists","reply_text":"Thank you, that is kind. There is a whole book behind these walks, {{cta_phrase_3}}"}

There is more than one right answer to every one of these. For the price objection above,
another shape entirely: "Understood. It is a beginner book that goes slowly, with a
chapter on the plants people confuse, and the decision is yours." Write the third yourself
and do not reuse either.

# Sourdough at Home: The Six Week Course

## What this is

An online course that takes a complete beginner from no starter at all to a repeatable
loaf in six weeks. Everything is pre-recorded and watched at your own pace. Nothing is
posted to you and there is nothing to collect.

## How it runs

Six weeks, one module a week. Each module is between forty and fifty minutes of video cut
into short lessons, plus a two page printable summary. Expect about two hours a week
including the baking itself. The modules stay available for as long as the subscription is
active and can be rewatched in any order.

Week one builds a starter from flour and water and explains what is happening in it.
Week two is the first loaf, deliberately a forgiving one.
Week three is shaping, and why most first loaves flatten.
Week four is timing, temperature and how to fit a bake around a working day.
Week five is the open crumb everyone is chasing, and the trade-offs it costs.
Week six is keeping a starter alive through neglect, holidays and a house move.

## What you need

A kitchen scale that reads in grams, a mixing bowl, a lidded oven-safe pot or a baking
tray, an oven that reaches 230 Celsius, and plain bread flour. No mixer, no proving
basket, no thermometer and no special container. Week one includes a section on baking in
a small or shared kitchen.

## Format and access

Video with English captions throughout. Spanish captions on weeks one to three, with the
rest in progress. Every printable summary is a PDF. There is a members forum where
questions are answered by the two people who made the course, usually within a day.

Video streams in a browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is no offline download
and no mobile app.

## What the course does not cover

Gluten-free and low-gluten baking, enriched doughs such as brioche, commercial or
wholesale scaling, wood-fired ovens, and selling what you bake. The course says plainly
that the last of those is a regulated business question and not a baking one.

## What is not in this document

Billing, cancellation, refunds, currency, gift subscriptions, team or classroom pricing,
and anything about someone's individual account. None of that is answered here.
